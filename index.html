Fragment-based quantum chemistry for complex molecular and materials systems

The XU-QCgroup develops quantum chemistry methods, molecular simulation workflows, and scientific software for studying complex chemical, biological, and materials systems. Our central goal is to extend accurate quantum-mechanical modeling to systems that are too large, heterogeneous, or electronically complex for conventional approaches.

Research

Method development

Effective fragment potential, effective fragment molecular orbital, and QM/EFP methods, with an emphasis on open-shell, multireference, and scalable formulations.

{% include figure.html src="" placeholder="Sequence → metal–peptide structure → binding free energy workflow" %}

Workflows and software

Automated workflows and user-friendly interfaces that make advanced fragmentation methods in GAMESS easier to use in high-throughput studies.

{% include figure.html src="" placeholder="Protein–ligand or host–guest binding example" %}

Applications

Metal–peptide coordination, protein–ligand binding, molecular recognition, solvation, and emerging problems in polymeric and nanoscale materials.
